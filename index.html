What CyberSploit adds

Not just collected — structured, linked, and completed.

Public PoCs are scattered across platforms, often lack CVE identifiers, and miss the information needed to reproduce them. CyberSploit addresses each of these gaps with a dedicated technique.

01

Multi-source collection

PoC reports from exploit databases, vulnerability advisories, mailing lists, bug trackers and code hosting — deduplicated, unified under one schema, and continuously expanded with new sources. PoCs without a CVE ID are kept and made searchable, instead of being dropped.

Methodology

From scattered reports to structured, actionable PoCs

Each release of CyberSploit is produced by the same four-stage pipeline. Results below are those reported in our papers.

STAGE 1

Collect

Incremental collection from diverse PoC sources; strict PoC filtering; cross-source deduplication.

Code, text, attachment and link-based PoCs
STAGE 2

Structure

LLM-based extraction of eight key aspects from heterogeneous code and free text.

P 0.96 · R 0.94
STAGE 3

Link

RESYNC recovers the CVE behind PoCs that do not cite one.

Recall@10 96.3% · MRR 0.85
STAGE 4

Complete

Missing aspects filled from CVE entries and complementary PoCs, after a behavioral-compatibility check.

Data access

Explore freely. Request full records for research.

Search and basic metadata are open. Full records — PoC code, trigger steps, verification oracles and attachments — are shared with researchers on request.

What is public

  • Key-aspect search over the dataset
  • Basic metadata: CVE, title, author, publish time, source platform
  • Which key aspects are available for each report

Responsible use

  • Data is collected only from publicly accessible pages, official data feeds and public web archives, at conservative request rates. No CAPTCHA solving, authentication bypass or non-public content.
  • Full records are provided for research and defensive purposes only, and may not be redistributed.
  • Content remains subject to the terms of its original platform. To request removal of a record, contact us.
